The garden, with the new ideas in

Seasons, a house you can paint and walk into, extras you earn, friends dropping by, watering, postcards and sound. You are Jo.

Try this

Tap the door, or the book. It swings open and you walk into a hallway where every finished idea hangs framed. Tap a frame to lean in. The back arrow takes you out.
Set Things done to 300+ below and go in again. Each wall is a stretch of time. Swipe sideways or use the arrows to walk back through it, and tap the list button, top right, to jump anywhere.
Tap Mini golf, the teal bloom with the 28 tag, and say "We did it!". Go inside again and it has its own frame. Do the same with Dumplings: the fifth thing done unlocks a birdbath.
Open Our garden, the round button left of "Pick for us". Paint the roof, walls and door, change the lawn, turn on sounds.
Tap Beach walk, the blue half-open flower at the front left. "Water to nudge Alex" tips the watering can.
Tap the camera for a postcard of today's garden.
Send a friend over with the button below and watch Alex's butterfly land.

Review controls

These are for review, not product UI. In the product the season follows the date, and a friend dropping by is a live event.

Reading a flower

A closed bud is new. A half-open cup is waiting on votes. A full bloom is everyone's in. A date marker behind the bloom means planned. Each flower has three leaves for Sam, Alex and Jo: a full leaf is "I'm in", a small leaf is "Eh", a green nub has not voted.
